About

Camera Keys was created by a photography teacher in downtown Los Angeles who teaches inner-city youth. The keychains are made to teach manual exposure and creative photo techniques — a small reference you can keep with the camera, so you can check a setting and keep shooting.

The cards

Three 3D-printed keychain cards to start. Each one is a compact flashcard for a camera setting or a shooting situation. The images are 3D-render placeholders, not product photos.
