Camera Keys

3D-printed flashcards for aperture, shutter speed, ISO, and the exposure triangle — made for photo students and hobbyists.

About

Camera Keys were created by a veteran photo teacher in Los Angeles Unified School District. The keychains teach manual exposure and creative photo techniques. Small reference you can keep with the camera so you can check a setting and keep shooting. Fast and on the go.

The cards

  • Sunny 16

    The sunny-16 rule: in bright sun, set the aperture to f/16 and the shutter to 1/ISO, then open up as the light falls, toward overcast.

  • Lighter

    Make an image lighter with more exposure: open the aperture (f/8 to f/5.6), slow the shutter (1/250 to 1/125), or raise the ISO (200 to 400). Each step doubles the light.

  • Darker

    Make an image darker with less exposure: close the aperture (f/5.6 to f/8), speed up the shutter (1/125 to 1/250), or lower the ISO (400 to 200). Each step halves the light.

    Coming soon

  • Exposure Triangle

    Aperture, shutter speed, and ISO work together. Change one by a stop and move another the opposite way to keep the same exposure: aperture sets depth of field, shutter sets motion, ISO sets noise.

    Coming soon

  • Studio with Strobe

    A studio starting point: ISO 100, shutter at your sync speed (often 1/200), and f/8. Adjust strobe power or aperture for the flash; the shutter mostly controls the ambient light.

    Coming soon

  • Low Light

    Shooting in low light: open up to f/2.8 or wider, keep the shutter at 1/focal length or faster handheld (1/60 for a 50mm), then raise the ISO to 1600–6400. Use a tripod for slower shutters.
